REVIVING RETRO TECH FOR A NEW GENERATION

Retrospekt is a highly specialized workshop and product design studio in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, committed to exploring, restoring and curating retro technology. 

Since 2008, Retrospekt has pursued vintage electronics and the delight of not-to-be-forgotten technology, breathing new life into cameras, portable cassette players, classic gaming consoles and other retro tech oddities.
